Privacy Policy — Dr. Anas Nehila Pharmacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

Dr. Anas Nehila Pharmacy (Anas Mohamed Ali Hassan Nehila — Commercial Register 67757), El Maamoura El Shatee, next to the main gate, Lavie Building, Alexandria, Egypt. Website: anasnehila.com.

Data we receive

When you comment on a post on our Facebook Page or send us a message, our system receives from Facebook: the text of your comment or message and the post it belongs to, your Facebook user ID and display name, and the time it was sent.

On our website we use Google Analytics for aggregated, anonymous visit statistics. Your cart and language choice are stored only on your own device (localStorage). Orders you send via WhatsApp reach us directly through WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To answer your question: if you ask about a product's price in a comment, we send you one private message with the price and product link, and a short public reply to your comment.
  • To fulfil and deliver your orders.
  • To understand which offers interest our customers so we can improve our service.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising outside our Page. We only message you in response to your own questions and orders.

Retention

  • The record that a comment was answered (comment ID only) is deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Post text is cached for up to 6 hours.
  • Conversations remain in our Page inbox on Facebook; you can delete them from your side at any time.

Who can access it

Only pharmacy staff. Data passes through the service providers we use — Meta (Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp), Cloudflare (the server that answers comments) and Google (analytics) — each under its own privacy policy.

Your rights and data deletion

You may ask what data we hold about you or request its deletion at any time via WhatsApp +20 128 882 7555, email info@anasnehila.com, or phone +20 3 5650501. We respond and delete within 30 days at most.
